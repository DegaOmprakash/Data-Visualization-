📊 Sales Data Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

This project is a Sales Data Dashboard built in Power BI to analyze company performance across different regions, products, and customer demographics.
The dashboard provides insights into Revenue, Profits, Orders, Product Categories, Discounts, and Demographics to support data-driven decision-making.

📷 Dashboard Preview

🔑 Key Insights

Total Revenue: 85M

Total Profits: 32M

Total Orders: 1M

1️⃣ Sales Trend Over Time

Overall revenue shows a decline from December to August.

2️⃣ Top Product Categories

🚲 Bikes dominate sales (62M).

🎒 Accessories contribute 15M.

👕 Clothing contributes 8M.

3️⃣ Profitability by Product

Bikes are the most profitable.

Accessories and Clothing have lower margins.

4️⃣ Discount vs Profit Relationship

Higher discounts negatively affect profit.

Controlled discounting improves profitability.

5️⃣ Regional Performance

North America leads in sales performance.

6️⃣ Customer Demographics

Gender split: ~50% Male vs 50% Female.

Majority buyers are Young Adults (25–34) and Adults (35–64).
